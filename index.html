The Endless Journey

Imagine we enter a boundless world – the world of colors. In this world there are 16,777,216 unique color nuances, each defined by its individual blend of red, green and blue (RGB). Each of these shades tells its own story, evokes its own emotion, is a little piece of magic.

The idea is as simple as it is fascinating: Every day we generate a completely random RGB hue and share it with you. A small moment of surprise that reminds us how diverse and beautiful the world around us is.

Why? Because it is a journey that will last for generations. A new shade every day – and it would take more than 45,000 years to post all RGB colors. It is a tribute to infinity and patience, a creative experiment that makes us pause and shows us how unique every moment, every day is.

Will you be part of this journey? Immerse yourself with us in the world of colors. Let yourself be surprised, inspired, and discover beauty in the seemingly simple – day by day, shade by shade.

"One small splash of color per day – for eternity."
